# 来源清单

## 核心研究与数据

1. Falchi, F. et al. (2016). *The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness*. Science Advances.  
   https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.1600377

2. Kyba, C. C. M. et al. (2023). *Citizen scientists report global rapid reductions in the visibility of stars from 2011 to 2022*. Science.  
   https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abq7781

3. NASA VIIRS Black Marble.  
   https://viirsland.gsfc.nasa.gov/Products/NASA/BlackMarble.html

4. Globe at Night.  
   https://globeatnight.org/

5. DarkSky International & Illuminating Engineering Society. *Five Principles for Responsible Outdoor Lighting*.  
   https://darksky.org/resources/guides-and-how-tos/lighting-principles/

6. McLaren, J. D. et al. (2018). *Artificial light at night confounds broad-scale habitat use by migrating birds*. PNAS.  
   https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1708574114

7. Horton, K. G. et al. (2023). Research on artificial light and migratory bird stopover density. Nature Communications.  
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-43046-z

## 使用规范

- 网页正文不长段复制来源文本，只做事实概述并链接原始来源。
- 数据文件在引入仓库时保留许可证、版本、下载日期和处理脚本。
- 每次更新地点数据后，在变更日志中记录模型版本及差异。
