# 《拆开一块电池》来源清单

访问日期：2026-07-28。

## 核心数据

1. U.S. Geological Survey. *Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026*. DOI: [10.3133/mcs2026](https://doi.org/10.3133/mcs2026).  
   用于：2025 年锂、钴、镍、天然石墨矿山产量与国家份额。

2. U.S. Geological Survey. *Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 Data Release*. DOI: [10.5066/P1WKQ63T](https://doi.org/10.5066/P1WKQ63T).  
   用于：机器可读数据发布与口径核对。

3. International Energy Agency. [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025). CC BY 4.0.  
   用于：2024 年矿山/精炼集中度、前三大精炼国 86%、供应链多元化与回收产能。

4. International Energy Agency. [Critical Minerals Data Explorer](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-tools/critical-minerals-data-explorer).  
   用于：矿物与供应链阶段的交叉核对。

## 电池化学与材料

5. International Energy Agency. [Material content in different anode and cathodes](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/charts/material-content-in-different-anode-and-cathodes).  
   用于：NMC、LFP 与石墨负极的材料构成。

6. International Energy Agency. [Global EV Outlook 2026 — Electric vehicle batteries](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-batteries).  
   用于：2025 年 LFP 与 NMC 平均成本差异的背景。

7. Argonne National Laboratory. [BatPaC Model Software](https://www.anl.gov/cse/batpac-model-software).  
   用于：参考电芯构成与工艺阶段的概念核对；页面未发布 BatPaC 的精确质量输出。

## 社会与供应链背景

8. International Labour Organization. [ILO launches GALAB project in the Democratic Republic of the Congo to address child labour](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/ilo-launches-galab-project-democratic-republic-congo-address-child-labour).  
   用于：刚果（金）采矿劳工背景；不用于估计整个国家或所有矿山的发生率。

9. United Nations Trade and Development. [Global Trade Update, June 2026: Shifting dynamics in critical minerals trade](https://unctad.org/publication/global-trade-update-june-2026-shifting-dynamics-critical-minerals-trade).  
   用于：关键矿物贸易与集中度背景。
